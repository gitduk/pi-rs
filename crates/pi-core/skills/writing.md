# Writing for a model

Write instructions that a model can interpret without clarification. Prioritize precision, completeness, and consistency over brevity.

## Rules

- Give each instruction one clear meaning.
- Use active voice and name the actor when it improves clarity.
- Keep one instruction per sentence.
- Prefer sentences under 20 words.
- Use consistent names for the same concept.
- Use direct verbs and concrete actions.
- Use lists when they clarify steps or conditions.
- Avoid unnecessary qualifiers, nominalizations, and stacked nouns.
- Split complex sentences instead of removing necessary details.

## Accuracy

- Preserve every meaningful condition, exception, limit, and number.
- Distinguish verified facts from assumptions and uncertainty.
- Do not invent causes, frequencies, or guarantees.
- Replace vague quality claims with observable criteria.
- Stop simplifying when further changes reduce precision.
